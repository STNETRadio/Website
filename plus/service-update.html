Important update

Closing the STNET Radio+ trial service

September 21, 2026 · STNET Radio+ Team

Hi everyone,

We have some news that we're sad to share. We're closing the STNET Radio+ trial on Apple Podcasts and Spotify. You can still subscribe until September 30, 2026 (Bangkok time), and after that, new sign-ups will close for good.

We started STNET Radio+ as a trial to see if a paid, extra-content version of the show could work. We learned a lot from it, mostly from you. This page explains what's happening, when, and what it means for you.

You can still subscribe until September 30, 2026 (Bangkok time).

The dates that matter

September 1, 2026

We stopped publishing new content and updates to STNET Radio+.

September 30, 2026

This is the last day to subscribe, by Bangkok time.

After that

Monthly and annual members keep their access until their current paid term ends.

What this means for you

If you're a member already, you don't need to do anything right now. You'll keep your access until the end of the term you've already paid for, whether that's a month or a year.

If you're thinking about joining, you can, until September 30. Just keep in mind that we've stopped adding new content, so you'd be joining for what's already there.

If you're not a member, nothing changes for you. Our free shows are still here, and you can find them on the Podcasts page.

Questions you might have

Why are you closing the trial?

It was always a trial, and it's now reached its end. We'd rather close it clearly and give you plenty of notice than let it fade out slowly.

Will I lose access straight away?

No. Members keep access until the end of their current paid term.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Your subscription is handled by Apple Podcasts or Spotify, depending on where you joined. You can check your renewal settings in your account with them.

I still have a question. Who can I ask?

Please get in touch through our Support page and we'll help.

Thank you for trying STNET Radio+ with us. Every listen, message and bit of feedback helped us, and we really are grateful.

This isn't goodbye.

We'll see you again in 2027.

With thanks,
The STNET Radio+ Team
